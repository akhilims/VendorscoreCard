AOA Community Transparency Charter

Ace Divino AOA ensures complete transparency. All vendor ratings are calculated from verified resident submissions with mandatory constructive feedback. Vendors below 2.5 average score over 60 days are automatically served contract termination notices.

Compulsory Feedback Policy

Every rating must be accompanied by a minimum 10-character written comment explaining specific observations to prevent arbitrary star dumping.

Sub-Contractor Accountability

Sub-vendors (like Pristine glass cleaning operating under CBRE) are scored separately so master management cannot hide sub-contractor deficiencies.

Penalty & Removal Rules

SLA violations logged by residents carry mandatory monetary fines deducted directly from the vendor's monthly AOA billing invoice.
